About

You're buying a person's judgment. Here's the person.

VAMA Consulting Group is a research-backed consulting practice founded in January 2021 by Andrija Popović. He leads every immigration documentation engagement and every AI operations audit himself.

An economist who writes things down

Andrija is a PhD candidate in Macroeconomics and a Research Assistant at the Innovation Center of the University of Niš. His research covers economic development, competitiveness, industrial development, and how businesses adopt new technology. It has been cited 107 times on Google Scholar.

Before founding VAMA, he worked in macroeconomic analysis, organizational optimization, business data analysis, and market research, including work connected to Bayer AG and the Faculty of Economics at the University of Niš.

That background shapes how VAMA works. Every projection has a source. Every assumption is written into an assumptions log. Every study states its method and its limits. It's the difference between a document that sounds confident and one that survives review.

Two practices, one standard

Immigration Business Documents. Since 2021 VAMA has supported 90+ documented U.S.-focused business, immigration, and strategic-planning engagements, representing $25M+ in documented U.S. investment planning. Immigration work spans E-2, L-1, EB-1C, EB-2 NIW, EB-1A, and RFE matters, for counsel - directly or white-label - and for applicants working with their attorneys.

AI Operations and Growth. The same discipline applied to operating businesses: measure the workflow first, price the delay, compare the fixes, and only then decide whether automation or AI earns a place.

How we handle your information

  • Nothing confidential goes through the website. Files are exchanged through a secure method agreed after the proposal.
  • We ask for exactly what the scope needs, from a checklist - not "send us everything".
  • Client data is placed in an AI tool only under an approved, written data mode. Restricted and no-external-AI modes are available.
  • No AI output reaches a client without human review.

Where and how we work

VAMA is registered in Niš, Serbia, and delivers remotely for U.S. clients and law firms, working across U.S. time zones. Working languages: English; Serbian, Spanish, and Portuguese on request.

VAMA is not a law firm and does not provide legal advice, filing strategy, or representation.

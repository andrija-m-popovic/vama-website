About

You are buying one person's judgment. Here is the person.

VAMA Consulting Group is a research-backed consulting practice founded in 2021 by Andrija Popović, who had been consulting independently since 2018. He leads every immigration documentation engagement and every workflow audit himself.

"Dr Action, not just Dr Theory."

Andrija finished a master's in economics and, in his words, "decided to put the hours I put into case study competitions and training teams into practice." His first stop was six months in regional coordination at the headquarters of a global pharmaceutical group, where one of his assignments was preparing the department's annual budget report, later used in departmental planning.

Back in Serbia he joined an EU medical-robotics consortium as the commercialization consultant for Eastern and South-Eastern Europe, producing the market-penetration strategy, the market-research studies and the product-placement reports. The consortium adopted his reports as the templates every partner had to use. He entered a PhD in macroeconomics at the same time and set himself a rule he still quotes: "Dr Action, not just Dr Theory."

He is a PhD candidate and research assistant at the Innovation Center of the University of Niš, with peer-reviewed publications on economic development, competitiveness and technology adoption in international journals. That research habit is the habit clients notice: every number has a source, every assumption is written down, and every limit is stated before anyone relies on it. Or, as he puts it, "Everything needs to be with the proof."

What the years of immigration work taught him

VAMA's immigration practice grew through white-label work for U.S. immigration firms and agencies, across E-2, L-1, EB-1C, EB-2 NIW and EB-1A matters. Two lessons from those years are now rules of the practice.

Consolidate the feedback. A financial model rebuilt four times because the client, the attorney and the agency each sent contradictory comments is a model nobody can trust. So inputs are locked before the model is built, and revisions come through one channel: "I want them to agree on what they will come back to me with."

Set the boundaries in the offer. Every proposal names the documents, the review terms, the timeline and the fee before work starts. Changed facts are a written extension. It protects the client's fee as much as it protects the work.

The proof that matters most to him is quieter than a testimonial: a renewal client who did not ask for "a business plan" but asked for him by name.

Why the AI practice exists

He has used AI in his own research and document work since the tools appeared, and builds his own automations. What he saw in the businesses around him was different: "Most SMEs don't have documented processes, and that is exactly where a business consultant can help." And: "It's not always that they need AI. With simple workflow automations they might get a lot better results in some cases."

So the AI practice is deliberately not an agency. It is a diagnostic. "The one thing I didn't want to do is be like big companies, where they give you strategy and big answers and then you figure out what to do and how to do it." The audit maps one workflow, measures it, compares the fixes on the same problem, and is allowed to recommend stopping.

How we handle your information

  • Nothing confidential goes through the website. Files are exchanged through a secure method agreed after the proposal.
  • We ask for exactly what the scope needs, from a checklist. Not "send us everything".
  • Client data is placed in an AI tool only under an approved, written data mode. Restricted and no-external-AI modes are available.
  • No AI output reaches a client without human review. "You are the one who needs to check."

Where and how we work

VAMA is registered in Niš, Serbia, and delivers remotely for U.S. clients and law firms across U.S. time zones: interviews by video, secure document exchange, written analysis, and a live debrief. Working languages: English; Serbian, Spanish and Portuguese on request.

VAMA is not a law firm and does not provide legal advice, filing strategy or representation.

What the documents need to make clear

Counsel preparing EB-1A petitions who want the forward-looking plan documented as business evidence.

The usual trouble spot: a strong record and a vague plan for what comes next, with no measures anyone could check in year 2.

Counsel decides what the petition argues. Our job is to make the business facts behind it specific, sourced, and consistent from the plan to the model to the org chart.

What not to send yet

Award certificates, publications, press, and letters. Counsel assembles the criteria evidence; we need only enough to plan the documents.

How every engagement is bounded

VAMA is not a law firm. We prepare business analysis and documents; counsel keeps legal strategy, eligibility decisions, petition arguments, and filing. Projections are planning estimates, not achieved results, and nothing we write is an assurance of approval.

The accepted proposal defines the documents, review rounds, formats, and timing. Changed facts, later RFE work, or new documents are quoted as a written scope extension - never as a surprise invoice.

EB-1A questions counsel asks us

Do you assess whether the applicant meets the criteria?

No. Eligibility and which criteria to argue are counsel's decisions. We document the forward-looking plan and its business context.

What does the performance-measurement annex contain?

The measures the applicant will track, a baseline for each, and targets for years 1 to 3, so the plan reads as specific and checkable.

Can this be combined with an EB-2 NIW filing?

Often, yes - many applicants file both. We keep the plans consistent while respecting how counsel frames each petition.

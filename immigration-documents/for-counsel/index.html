How every engagement is bounded

VAMA is not a law firm. We prepare business analysis and documents; counsel keeps legal strategy, eligibility decisions, petition arguments, and filing. Projections are planning estimates, not achieved results, and nothing we write is an assurance of approval.

The accepted proposal defines the documents, review rounds, formats, and timing. Changed facts, later RFE work, or new documents are quoted as a written scope extension - never as a surprise invoice.

Questions firms ask before a pilot

Do you ever contact our client directly?

Only in the way you set. Some firms want every exchange to go through them; others let us run the fact questionnaire with the client. You decide at the workflow call, and we stick to it.

Can the documents carry our firm's branding?

Yes. We deliver branded to your template or fully unbranded, whichever fits your practice.

How are wholesale fees set?

Per document or package, fixed before work starts, with volume bands for firms that send regular matters. Terms are shared privately with firms and are never published.

What does a pilot commit us to?

One matter. No volume commitment, no retainer. After the debrief you decide whether to send the next one.

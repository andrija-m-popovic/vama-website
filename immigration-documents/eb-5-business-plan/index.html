About our method

Our job-creation studies use a BLS-based Type I input-output method (direct plus indirect effects). If a matter requires a specific licensed model such as RIMS II or IMPLAN, tell us at scope - we will say plainly whether we can meet that requirement before anyone commits.

How every engagement is bounded

VAMA is not a law firm. We prepare business analysis and documents; counsel keeps legal strategy, eligibility decisions, petition arguments, and filing. Projections are planning estimates, not achieved results, and nothing we write is an assurance of approval.

The accepted proposal defines the documents, review rounds, formats, and timing. Changed facts, later RFE work, or new documents are quoted as a written scope extension - never as a surprise invoice.

EB-5 questions counsel asks us

Do you work on regional center projects?

We focus on direct EB-5 enterprises. For regional center projects we assess the requested scope with counsel before accepting, because specialist modeling requirements vary.

Which job-creation method will you use?

A BLS-based Type I method by default, with direct and indirect effects and no induced effects. If counsel needs a specific licensed model, we say at scope whether we can deliver it.

Do you guarantee a job count?

No. Job figures are planning estimates from the plan's assumptions and the stated method. We show the inputs so counsel can test them.

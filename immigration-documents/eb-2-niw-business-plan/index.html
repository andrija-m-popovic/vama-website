  • The applicant's CV and a short description of the proposed work

  • Any funding, clients, partners, or letters of interest already in place
  • Counsel's preferred framing, if already decided

Recommendation letters, publications, and the full petition draft. At scope we only need enough to size the work.

How every engagement is bounded

VAMA is not a law firm. We prepare business analysis and documents; counsel keeps legal strategy, eligibility decisions, petition arguments, and filing. Projections are planning estimates, not achieved results, and nothing we write is an assurance of approval.

The accepted proposal defines the documents, review rounds, formats, and timing. Changed facts, later RFE work, or new documents are quoted as a written scope extension - never as a surprise invoice.

EB-2 NIW questions counsel asks us

What method do your economic impact studies use?

A BLS-based Type I input-output method: direct plus indirect effects, reported as output and job-years. We do not report induced effects. Each study states its sources, boundaries, and limits so counsel can explain them.

Can the plan be written for a researcher rather than a founder?

Yes. For a founder the plan centers on the company. For a researcher or practitioner it centers on the work program, who uses the results, and how the work is funded.

Do you write the petition letter?

No. The petition letter is your work. The exhibits behind it are ours.

RFE business-document audits: find every gap before anyone rewrites

When an RFE questions the business plan, the financials, or the economic analysis, we audit the existing documents first, map each issue, and then repair only what the evidence requires.

What the audit checks

Counsel responding to an RFE that touches business plans, financial models, org charts, or impact studies - including documents another provider prepared.

The usual trouble spot: rewriting the paragraph the RFE quoted, and creating a new inconsistency two exhibits away.

Counsel decides what the petition argues. Our job is to make the business facts behind it specific, sourced, and consistent from the plan to the model to the org chart.

ConsistencyDo the plan, model, org chart, and impact figures agree with each other?
SupportWhich assumptions have no source, and which sources are out of date?
CoverageEach business-document point in the RFE, mapped to the pages that answer it - or don't.
ArithmeticJobs, payroll, revenue, and investment figures, recalculated.

What we need to scope it

  • The RFE's business-document points (the relevant section is enough at scope)
  • The filed business plan, financial model, and any impact study
  • The response due date

What not to send yet

The full petition and personal records. At scope we only need the business-document points and the due date.

Timeline and revisions

Timing is fixed in the proposal against your filing date. Our standard path once intake is complete: evidence intake on days 1-3, draft and internal QA on days 4-7, counsel review and a clean final package by day 10. One consolidated revision round is included. RFE clocks are fixed. Put the response due date in your scope request - if it is under 14 days away, we confirm capacity before anything else.

How every engagement is bounded

VAMA is not a law firm. We prepare business analysis and documents; counsel keeps legal strategy, eligibility decisions, petition arguments, and filing. Projections are planning estimates, not achieved results, and nothing we write is an assurance of approval.

The accepted proposal defines the documents, review rounds, formats, and timing. Changed facts, later RFE work, or new documents are quoted as a written scope extension - never as a surprise invoice.

RFE questions counsel asks us

Can you audit documents another provider prepared?

Yes. Most audits start from someone else's file. We assess it on the evidence, not on who wrote it.

Will you respond to the RFE?

No. Counsel owns the legal response. We audit and repair the business documents behind it and give counsel an issue matrix to work from.

Why audit if we already know what the RFE says?

The RFE names the symptom. The audit checks whether the same problem appears elsewhere in the file, so a targeted rewrite does not create a new inconsistency.

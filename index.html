The usual routes for a business plan

A plan mill is fast and generic: the numbers rarely tie out between the narrative, the model and the org chart, and nobody wrote down where they came from.

The client's accountant knows the history but does not write forward-looking plans for counsel's review.

Writing it in-house spends attorney hours on a non-legal document.

Here: one economist leads the file from intake to handover. Sources named and dated, an assumptions log, an editable model, and the legal-boundary language stated once, clearly.

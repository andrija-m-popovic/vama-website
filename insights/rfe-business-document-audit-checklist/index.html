The RFE business-document audit: what to check before anyone rewrites

The RFE names a symptom. The plan, the model, the org chart, and the impact study are connected - change one number and three others can break. Audit first.

Andrija Popović 25 September 20265 min read

When an RFE questions the business plan, the financial projections, or the economic analysis, the natural instinct is to rewrite the section it names. That's often the wrong first move.

Business documents in a petition are connected. The revenue in the plan comes from the model. The payroll in the model comes from the org chart. The impact study is built on both. Rewrite one section in isolation and you can fix the paragraph the RFE quoted while creating a new inconsistency two exhibits away.

So we audit first. Here's what that means in practice.

What the audit checks

1. Coverage: each point, mapped to a page

We list every business-document point the RFE raises and map it to the pages that currently address it - or note that nothing does. This alone often shows the problem isn't missing content but content that is hard to find or buried in an appendix.

2. Arithmetic: the numbers, recalculated

Jobs, payroll, revenue, investment totals, and impact figures are recalculated across every document. Totals that don't match between the plan and the model are among the easiest issues for a reviewer to spot, and the easiest for us to fix.

3. Sources: named, dated, and current

Every market figure, wage, and multiplier should have a named source and a date. We flag unsourced figures and sources that have since been updated.

4. Consistency: names, dates, headcounts, titles

Entity names, start dates, role titles, and headcounts should match across the plan, the model, the org chart, and the impact study. Small differences read as carelessness, or worse, as uncertainty about the facts.

5. Support: assumptions with nothing behind them

Growth rates without a driver, prices without a comparison, hiring without a reason. Each is listed with what would support it, or a recommendation to change it.

6. The exhibit trail

Does every factual claim in the plan point to an exhibit or a source? If not, the exhibit index becomes part of the repair.

Then repair - only what the evidence requires

With the matrix agreed, the repair is targeted: revised sections, a corrected model, updated org charts, a refreshed market section or impact study where needed, and a new exhibit index. Because it's scoped from the audit, the fee is fixed before the rewrite starts, and nothing is rewritten just to look busy.

Where the line sits

The legal response to the RFE is counsel's work. We audit and repair the business documents behind it, and hand counsel a matrix that makes the legal response easier to write.

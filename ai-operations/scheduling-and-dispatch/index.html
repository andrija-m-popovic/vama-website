Sound familiar?

  • Reschedules ripple through phone calls and text threads
  • Job notes live in a technician's phone or memory
  • Office staff copy the same job details into scheduling, invoicing, and customer messages
  • Invoices go out days after the job is finished

Best fit: Teams that schedule technicians, crews, or appointments on a calendar that changes every day.

We measure it first, then compare the fixes on the same problem.

Most of the value is in the order: find out what the delay actually costs, check what your current software already does, and only then decide whether automation or AI earns its place.

What we measure

  • Touches per job, from booking to invoice
  • Lag between job completion and invoice
  • Reschedule rate and its causes
  • Hours spent on status updates and duplicate entry

Options we compare

  • Features already in your field-service or scheduling software
  • Standard job-note templates captured on site
  • Automated confirmations and on-the-way messages to customers
  • An integration that drafts the invoice when a job is closed

What we usually leave to people

  • Dispatch decisions that depend on a technician's skills and the customer's history
  • Exception calls with an unhappy customer

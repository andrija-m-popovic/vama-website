Sound familiar?

  • Each location follows up differently - or not at all
  • Leads move between locations by forwarded email
  • Leadership sees the numbers once a month, rebuilt by hand
  • What works at one site never reaches the others

Best fit: Businesses with 2 or more locations, branches, or territories that share leads, customers, or reporting.

What the audit does here

We measure it first, then compare the fixes.

Most of the value is in the order: find out what the delay actually costs, check what your current software already does, and only then decide whether automation or AI earns its place.

What we measure

  • Follow-up rate and speed by location
  • Lead hand-off time between locations
  • Hours spent assembling reports
  • Conversion differences between locations

Options we compare

  • One lead-routing rule for every location
  • A shared follow-up cadence with an owner at each location
  • Automated weekly reporting from the systems you already use
  • AI summaries of call notes or reviews, checked by a manager

What we usually leave to people

  • Local relationship decisions
  • Staff performance conversations

The offer

Same audit, focused on your multi-location follow-up workflow.

You get a priced, written answer to one question: which part of this workflow is worth fixing first, and how?

  1. Diagnose

    Follow the workflow from the customer's first contact to the business outcome.

  2. Validate

    Confirm the baseline and separate cash cost, capacity, and revenue opportunity.

  3. Prioritize

    Score the options - and name what should not be automated.

  4. Roadmap

    Define a small pilot, its owner, how success is tested, and how to roll back if needed.

"We want to use AI" is not a brief.

Growth has added another job to the owner's desk. Enquiries get routed by hand. Quotes go out and the next step depends on someone remembering. Job details get typed into three systems. And every vendor conversation starts with the software, not the workflow.

Nobody can say what a slow reply costs.

Nobody can say how many quotes went quiet last month.

So the tool gets bought on a hunch, and the workflow underneath it stays the same.

The audit starts one step earlier: what starts the task, who handles each step, what information they need, where the work waits or comes back for correction, and what better would look like.

How this is different from the other offers in your inbox.

What you have probably been offered

The free AI assessment. A sales call with a report attached. It ends with the tool the seller already chose.

The automation agency. Paid to build, so the recommendation is a build.

The chatbot demo. Impressive for 20 minutes. Silent on what it costs to supervise, and on the 1 call in 200 it gets wrong.

What this is

A paid, tool-independent diagnostic. The fee covers a defined analysis and roadmap, not a discovery call or a list of tools.

The options are compared on the same problem. A process change, a feature you already pay for, conventional automation, or an AI-assisted step, in that order.

A useful audit can recommend stopping. If the right answer is a reminder you already own, or no project at all, that is what the roadmap says. Implementation is scoped separately, with us or with anyone.

  • Customers wait for replies, quotes or scheduling, repeatedly

Not a fit, and we say so on the call

  • Nobody who does the work can spare an interview
  • You want a chatbot live by next week
  • You need a U.S.-based team on site rather than remote delivery

Value leaks at the handoffs.

Not inside the software. In the wait between a call and a reply, the quote nobody owns, and the job details typed into three systems. A missed call may begin in one tool, become a note in another, wait in an inbox, and disappear before anyone owns the next action. That is where the audit starts.

Business clarity first. Technology second.

  1. Diagnose

    Follow the workflow from the customer's first contact to the business outcome. Compare what people describe with the records you already have.

  2. Validate

    Confirm the baseline with your data, and separate cash cost, capacity and revenue opportunity.

  3. Prioritize

    Score the options: a process change, a feature you already pay for, automation, or AI. Name what should not be automated.

  4. Roadmap

    Define a small pilot: who owns it, how success is tested, and how to roll back if needed.

Questions operators ask

Is this limited to particular industries?

No. Fit is determined by the workflow, not the sector. Typical examples: field services, trades, property services, professional and advisory firms, healthcare administration, logistics, construction, and multi-location businesses.

You are in Serbia. How does that work?

The audit is designed for remote delivery: interviews by video, secure document exchange, written analysis, and a live executive debrief. Your team keeps control of its systems, data access and implementation decisions. If U.S.-based physical presence is a requirement, we are not the right fit, and we will say so on the call.

Do you promise savings or new revenue?

No. We show the inputs, assumptions, confidence ranges and a measurement plan so you can judge the numbers yourself. Anyone guaranteeing savings before seeing your workflow is guessing.

Will our information be uploaded to AI tools?

Only under the written data mode you approve. Restricted and no-external-AI modes are available, data is minimized, and no AI output is delivered without human review.

Can we implement the roadmap ourselves?

Yes. The audit is a standalone deliverable. Use it with your team or any provider. If you want our help with a pilot, that is a separate, written proposal.

Is this just a sales call for software?

No. The audit is tool-independent. We have no reseller arrangement to protect, and the roadmap can recommend a feature you already pay for, or no project at all.

Is the 20-minute fit call a free version of the audit?

No, and we say so up front. The call is a fit check: we hear about one workflow and tell you honestly whether an audit is worth it. No interviews, analysis or roadmap are included.

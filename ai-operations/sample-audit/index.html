6 · What we recommend not automating, and why

  • Pricing of non-standard jobs. It depends on the owner's judgment of site conditions and margin. Automating it moves risk without removing work.
  • The first call from property managers. Few in number, high in lifetime value. A person should answer.
  • An AI phone agent for all calls. About 30% of calls are emergencies. Misrouting one costs more than a year of the leak it would fix.

8 · The one-page decision sheet

Do

  • Name a follow-up owner this week
  • Turn on the reminders you already pay for
  • Standardize visit notes

Don't

  • Buy an AI phone agent
  • Automate non-standard pricing
  • Add a new CRM - the current software is enough

Later

  • AI quote summaries, once notes are structured
  • Review the close-rate lift at day 90
  • Consider a second workflow: scheduling
